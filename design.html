UKRAINE · THE BRIDGE WAR

Kerch arch falls into the sea as Kyiv’s bridges are hit

Ukraine strikes the road-and-rail link to Crimea a second straight day; the road arch collapses into the strait and traffic halts, rail traffic reported running. Hours earlier, Russian missiles hit two of Kyiv’s main bridges over the Dnipro, closing both.

THE GULF · BAHRAIN

Iran’s drones target US radar — again

Tehran claims a hit on the radar at Isa Air Base, its third attack in five days. Bahrain and US Central Command say every drone was intercepted: no damage, no injuries.

The Quad meets for the first time in two years. Today Trump holds a state visit with Marcos, then flies on for Thursday’s first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin meeting.
